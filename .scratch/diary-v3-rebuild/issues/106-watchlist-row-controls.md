# [106] Reduce the Watchlist row to a readable company, not six buttons

Status: needs-triage
Execution: todo
Published: 2026-10-06

Category: bug
Type: Design follow-up
User stories covered: US — Watchlist. Keep companies close while developing an investment view.

## Observation

Found during the [whole-app page score](../../../docs/design/ui-page-score-2026-10-06.md),
where `/stocks/watchlist` scored **48/100** and had the worst row-control density in the
app at both widths.

**Six controls on every row, all at full button weight:** View research (secondary
button), an icon-only pencil, Move up, Move down, Pin, Remove. Four companies means
**24 buttons on one screen**, occupying roughly 500px of a 1160px row. At 390px they wrap
to two rows of three buttons per company, so the control block is taller than the company
information it acts on.

`DESIGN.md` is direct about this: quiet (`.quiet-button`) is "for row-level controls that
must not compete," and the Do's and Don'ts ask for row controls at quiet weight. The
delivered `/discipline` page — scored 86 in the same review — is the in-repo model: icon-only
reorder at quiet weight, a text Edit, and a single danger control.

**The empty text is printed twice per row.** `routes/watchlist.tsx:47` and `:49` both
fall back to `c.none` ("No research records yet."), in two adjacent columns. Every
unresearched company shows the sentence twice. Confirmed in source.

**Three stacked bordered boxes precede any content:** a three-up stat row (Companies /
With research / Not yet researched), a "Quick add" card, then a filter card. The reader
passes through three edges before reaching the list. `DESIGN.md`: "`.panel` is the default
way to group content … flush on the canvas — no box," and cards are for "a form, a dialog
body, or content that genuinely needs an edge."

**Disabled controls add noise rather than information.** Move up is disabled on the first
row and Move down on the last, so two greyed buttons are always present among the 24.

**Orphaned helper text.** "Move up and down is available in custom order." sits between
the filter card and the list, attached to neither.

**"Refresh watchlist" floats above an unheaded region** — the same pattern
[112](112-empty-states-and-list-sections.md) covers across four pages.

## Requested outcome

Make a watchlist row read as a company with its latest research, with its management
controls present but subordinate. Preserve every capability: custom ordering, pinning,
research filter, search, inline symbol editing, removal with undo, and the 100-company
bound.

## Implementation plan

**1. Quiet the row.** The primary row affordance is the company and its latest research
summary. "View research" stays as the one visible action; reorder, pin, edit and remove
become quiet-weight — icon-only where `/discipline` has already established the pattern
and the accessible name is carried properly.

**2. Decide what the row shows versus what it hides.** Six controls per row is beyond the
four-item working-memory guideline. Candidates: group reorder/pin/edit/remove behind a
per-row disclosure, or show reorder only when the sort is Custom order (which is already
the documented condition in that orphaned helper line, and would delete the line). This
is the decision triage needs to make — the review did not conclude that a `More actions`
menu is right, only that six peers are wrong. Note `c.more` ("More actions") already
exists in `watchlist-copy.ts`, suggesting this was once intended.

**3. Delete the duplicate column.** Fix `watchlist.tsx:47`/`:49` so an unresearched row
states it once. Shared with [102](102-confirmed-markup-defects.md) item 3 — whichever
ships first owns it.

**4. Collapse the three leading boxes.** The counts become a ledger line or a sentence
under the lede; Quick add and the filters become panels grouped by a rule, or Quick add
moves beside the heading as a single field-plus-submit.

**5. Attach or delete the helper text**, per the step 2 decision.

**6. Handle disabled reorder honestly** — either omit the control at the boundary or keep
it disabled with an accessible explanation, but not as a third greyed peer.

**Verification:** `tests/e2e/library-watchlist-improvements.spec.ts` and any watchlist
coverage in the library specs are updated and must pass, with added assertions for the
single empty-text instance, for reorder remaining operable by keyboard at whatever
disclosure level step 2 chooses, and for removal-with-undo surviving the restructure.

## Provisional acceptance criteria

- [ ] A watchlist row's most prominent element is the company and its latest research, not its controls.
- [ ] Row management controls are quiet-weight; no row renders more than one full-weight button.
- [ ] At most four controls are visible per row at 1440 and at 390; any others sit behind one disclosure with an accessible name.
- [ ] An unresearched row states "No research records yet." exactly once.
- [ ] At most one bordered region precedes the list; counts, quick add and filters are grouped by rules and space.
- [ ] The orphaned custom-order helper line is either attached to the control it describes or removed along with the condition that required it.
- [ ] Custom ordering, pinning, search, research filter, inline symbol edit, remove-with-undo and the 100-company bound all behave as before.
- [ ] Reorder and remove are fully operable by keyboard, with state changes announced.
- [ ] At 390px a company's control block is not taller than its information.
- [ ] Verified in all three locales, light and dark, at 390/768/1440.
