# [110] Section the company research page into legible jobs

Status: needs-triage
Execution: todo
Published: 2026-10-06

Category: enhancement
Type: Design follow-up
User stories covered: US — Company research hub. Quote, history, position, thesis, notes and research evidence for one symbol.

## Observation

Found during the [whole-app page score](../../../docs/design/ui-page-score-2026-10-06.md),
where `/stocks/:symbol` scored **58/100**.

**Eight distinct jobs occupy one 3,354px scroll** with no in-page navigation: symbol
lookup, your position, current thesis view, original decisions, later reviews, latest
quote, historical closing prices, company notes (with two filter selects and pagination),
a research-evidence capture form, and the evidence timeline. Each is independently useful
and each has its own loading and failure state, which is good engineering — but there is
no way to get to one of them without scrolling past the others.

**Four filled buttons appear down the page**: "Record a thought", "View market data",
"New note", "Capture evidence" — plus the secondary "Write a full diary", "Refresh
quote", "Refresh notes". `DESIGN.md` wants one filled action per surface; with eight jobs
stacked, each contributing its own primary, the page has no primary at all.

**Precision is wrong on the figures that matter most.** `Portfolio share · cost basis`
renders `74.976878%`; `Change` renders `+10.000000` and `Change percent` `+10.000000%`.
Six decimal places on a percentage and on a price change. Owned by
[101](101-figure-formatting-consistency.md), but this page is its worst instance and the
reason the review ranked 101 first.

**Pagination renders for an empty set.** With no notes, the page shows "No notes yet."
*and* disabled "Previous notes" / "Next notes" buttons with "1 / 1" between them.

**The notes filters break the page's own label convention.** "Whose notes" and "Note
source" render as labels to the *left* of their selects, while every other field on the
page — and in the app — puts the label above. Two label positions on one page.

**The research-evidence form is always expanded.** Source type, Occurred at, Evidence
summary, Source title and Source URL are five empty controls sitting open in the middle
of a reading surface, below the thesis and above the evidence timeline, whether or not the
reader intends to capture anything.

The page's content model is right and should be preserved: `DESIGN.md` records that
"company pages separate current views from follow-up evidence," and they do. The defect is
density and the absence of a primary, not the information architecture.

## Requested outcome

Make a company legible at a glance and each of its jobs reachable without scrolling the
whole record. Preserve every section's independent loading, failure and retry behaviour,
the quote-missing semantics, the 50-row history cap, and the immutability of saved
evidence.

## Implementation plan

**1. Decide the sectioning mechanism.** Candidates: in-page navigation over the existing
sections; promote the heaviest regions (notes, evidence) to their own routes under
`/stocks/:symbol/*`, as `thesis` already is; or defer the capture-oriented regions behind
disclosures that open when they already hold content — the pattern
[97](97-quick-composer-redesign.md) established for the diary editor and that this
codebase already knows how to do. The third is the cheapest and most consistent with
delivered work. **Triage decides.**

**2. Establish one primary per view.** Whatever the sectioning, each resulting view has
one filled action. "Record a thought" is the likeliest page-level primary given the
product's diary-first principle.

**3. Defer the evidence capture form.** It becomes a disclosure that opens when it has
content, matching the diary editor's deferred regions.

**4. Fix the notes region.** Labels above fields like everywhere else; suppress pagination
when there is one page or none; keep the two filters but group them as one control row.

**5. Let [101](101-figure-formatting-consistency.md) own the figures.** Do not
re-implement formatting here; this page is one of its call sites.

**Verification:** `tests/e2e/company-hub.spec.ts` and `company-market.spec.ts` are updated
and must pass. Note the [2026-10-02 changelog](../../../docs/design/CHANGELOG.md) records
eight pre-existing failures in `company-market`, `portfolio`, `portfolio-exposure` and
`trade-plans`, confirmed against an unmodified tree at the time. Establish whether those
are still failing *before* starting, so this work is not blamed for them and does not hide
them.

## Provisional acceptance criteria

- [ ] Each of the page's jobs is reachable without scrolling past the others, by whichever mechanism triage rules.
- [ ] Each resulting view has exactly one filled action.
- [ ] The research-evidence capture form is deferred and opens itself when it already holds content.
- [ ] Notes filters place labels above their controls, consistent with the rest of the page and the app.
- [ ] Pagination controls do not render for an empty or single-page set.
- [ ] No percentage or price change renders more than two decimal places (via 101).
- [ ] Per-section loading, failure and retry remain independent; one failing section never blocks another.
- [ ] Missing quotes stay labelled and are never rendered as `0`; the 50-row history cap and the range select are unchanged.
- [ ] Saved evidence remains immutable and the evidence timeline order is unchanged.
- [ ] The pre-existing `company-market` failures are characterised before work starts and are neither introduced nor masked by it.
- [ ] Verified in all three locales, light and dark, all three market-colour preferences, at 390/768/1440.
