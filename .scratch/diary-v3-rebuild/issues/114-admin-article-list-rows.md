# [114] Quiet the article management rows and size its columns

Status: needs-triage
Execution: todo
Published: 2026-10-06

Category: bug
Type: Design follow-up
User stories covered: Article management — drafts, publication, and the public article index.

## Observation

Found during the [whole-app page score](../../../docs/design/ui-page-score-2026-10-06.md),
where `/admin/blog` scored **58/100**. The naming defects on this page are filed as
[102](102-confirmed-markup-defects.md) item 4; this ticket covers what is left, which is
the table itself.

**Row actions are at full weight and wrap to a second line.** `routes/admin-blog.tsx:110`
renders, per row: Edit (secondary link-button), View article (secondary, published only),
Archive or Publish, and Delete (secondary danger). On a published row that is four
buttons, and at 1440px they wrap so Delete drops onto its own line below the other three —
the row's controls become two rows tall. `DESIGN.md` asks for quiet-weight row controls
so they do not compete.

**"Publish" is a filled button inside a table row.** Same line: the publish branch renders
`<button type="button" onClick={…}>{c.publish}</button>` with no `className`, so it takes
the default filled style, while every sibling control is `secondary`. The page already has
a filled primary — "New article", top right — so a draft row now carries a second filled
action competing with it. A published row has no filled control at all, so the visual
weight of a row varies with its status for no stated reason.

**The Status column is too narrow for its content.** "Published" breaks mid-word as
"Publishe / d" at 1440px. The column is sized by the grid rather than by its longest
label, and the label set is known and short.

**The bulk bar leads with a disabled filled button.** "0 selected" sits beside a greyed
filled "Publish selected" and a greyed "Delete selected", so the resting state of the page
shows a disabled primary. Covered as a convention question by
[102](102-confirmed-markup-defects.md) item 6; the ruling there applies here.

The page's capabilities are fine and the state model is the one
`DESIGN.md` specifies — Draft/Published/Archived each exposing one primary plus the stated
separate action. The defect is that the row expresses it with four peers at button weight.

## Requested outcome

Make an article row read as an article with its status, with its management controls
subordinate and non-wrapping, and give the status column enough room for its labels.
Preserve every action, the bulk publish and delete paths, and the access-level semantics.

## Implementation plan

**1. Quiet the row controls.** Edit, View, Archive/Publish and Delete become quiet-weight.
Delete keeps `danger` semantics and its confirmation. No row control is filled.

**2. Decide how the per-state primary reads.** `DESIGN.md` requires one filled primary per
article state, but that rule was written for the article *editor*, where the article is
the page. In a list, "one filled action per row" multiplies into one per row. The
resolution is probably that the list has no filled row actions and the editor keeps its
primary — but that is a reading of the rule and should be recorded as a ruling rather
than assumed.

**3. Size the Status column to its longest label** so no status breaks mid-word, in all
three locales — the zh-TW and zh-CN labels differ in width from the English ones.

**4. Stop the action group wrapping** at 1440px; if four controls cannot fit once quieted,
group the secondary ones behind a per-row disclosure, consistent with whatever
[106](106-watchlist-row-controls.md) rules for watchlist rows.

**Verification:** `tests/e2e/posts.spec.ts` and `article-publish-feedback.spec.ts` are
updated and must pass. Add an assertion that no status label wraps and that the row action
group occupies one line at 1440px, in English and zh-TW.

## Provisional acceptance criteria

- [ ] No row control on `/admin/blog` is filled; Delete keeps its danger semantics and confirmation.
- [ ] A row's control group occupies one line at 1440px in all three locales, or its extras sit behind one disclosure.
- [ ] Rows have consistent visual weight regardless of article status.
- [ ] No status label breaks mid-word in any locale.
- [ ] The per-state-primary rule is ruled on for lists versus editors, and DESIGN.md is corrected if the rule's scope changes.
- [ ] Edit, View, Publish, Archive, Delete, bulk publish and bulk delete all behave as before, with confirmations intact.
- [ ] Draft/Published/Archived semantics and the PUBLIC/MEMBER access select are unchanged.
- [ ] Verified in all three locales, light and dark, at 390/768/1440.
