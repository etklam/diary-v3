## Observation

Found during the [whole-app page score](../../../docs/design/ui-page-score-2026-10-06.md).
Each item below was confirmed in source, is one to a few lines, and is independent of
every other item. They are bundled because splitting six two-line fixes into six tickets
costs more to triage than to fix.

**1. "Review dueJan 1, 2020" — missing space.** `apps/web/app/routes/diary.tsx:150`:

```jsx
{reviewOverdue ? <strong>{reviewLabels.dueNow}</strong> : `${reviewLabels.duePrefix} `}<time …>
```

The non-overdue branch ends its string with a space; the overdue branch does not. Every
overdue diary reading page concatenates the label into the date. Visible in all three
locales.

**2. Overview's portfolio figures are not in the monospace cut.**
`apps/web/app/overview.css:268` sets `.overview-metrics dd { font-variant-numeric:
tabular-nums }` but no `font-family`, so Priced market value, Quote coverage and Unpriced
cost basis render in IBM Plex Sans at weight 650. `styles.css:112` defines the mono cut
for `.num`/`.figure` and `styles.css:672` applies it correctly in `.stat-value`; this one
selector misses it. The concentration percentage in the attention list has the same
problem for a different reason — it is a bare `<span>` at `overview.tsx:148` with no
figure class. This breaks the Figure Rule on the most-visited page in the app.

**3. Watchlist prints its empty text twice per row.**
`apps/web/app/routes/watchlist.tsx:47` and `:49` both fall back to `c.none`
("No research records yet."), in two adjacent columns. Every unresearched company shows
the sentence twice, at 1440 and at 390.

**4. "Article management" is printed three times on `/admin/blog`.**
`apps/web/app/routes/admin-blog.tsx:98` renders `{c.title}` as the `h1`; `:110` renders
it again as the table `<caption>` *and* again as the first column's `<th>`. The title
column's header is therefore the page's own name. Same line also renders
`{row.author.name ?? '—'}`, so a missing author prints "By —" above the email.

**5. The thesis invalidation checkbox is not associated with its label.**
`/stocks/:symbol/thesis` renders the "Invalidation condition triggered" checkbox centred
on its own line with the label text on the following line, left-aligned — control and
label neither adjacent nor on a shared baseline. Needs the layout checked against the
Fields rule, and the `label`/`for` association verified while there.

**6. Disabled filled buttons used to signal invalid input.** `DESIGN.md`'s Buttons
section is explicit: "Disabled is .5 opacity with a waiting cursor, because buttons here
are disabled while a submit is in flight rather than to signal invalid input." Four
surfaces ship a greyed filled button as the resting state: `/diaries/quick` ("Create
diary" while the textarea is empty), `/admin/blog` ("Publish selected" at 0 selected),
`/admin/article-translations` ("Apply provider" with no provider), and `/trade-plans/:id`
("Save execution selection" with nothing selected). Either the rule is wrong or these
are; triage should pick one.

**7. Clipped row actions on `/tools/relative-value`.** The scenario table's action
buttons are cut off by the panel's right edge and read **"Use this ro"**. Visible at
1440px, so this is not a narrow-viewport problem.
