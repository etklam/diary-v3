# [116] Decide what belongs in the sidebar

Status: needs-triage

## Observation

Recorded in the [whole-app page score](../../../docs/design/ui-page-score-2026-10-06.md)
and carried in [112](112-empty-states-and-list-sections.md)'s Observation as deliberately
unfiled pending a ruling. Filed here by [112](112-empty-states-and-list-sections.md)'s
execution rather than dropped.

**The sidebar's content measures 1,745px.** It scrolls correctly — `overflow-y` computes
to `auto`, so nothing is unreachable — but at 1440×1080 it leaves **665px below its own
fold**, including **Settings, Sign out and Preferences**. With roughly 30 destinations in
the tree, finding one means scrolling a column that does not look like it scrolls.

This is an information-architecture question, not a consistency defect: the fix is a
decision about what belongs in a persistent sidebar at all, and it interacts with the
standing rule that navigation must serve capture → read → review before anything else.
`DESIGN.md` already records the grouping (Overview, then Diary & review, Investing &
trading, Markets & tools, then Diary management, Trade management, Account,
Administration) and the command palette already exists above the list precisely because
"the route count exceeds what any sidebar can hold" — which is the problem stated in a
comment rather than solved.

## Requested outcome

A sidebar whose standing content fits the viewport it is designed for, with every
destination still reachable, and with the account controls — Settings, Sign out,
Preferences — not below a fold.

## Implementation plan

**1. Measure before deciding.** Record the current height, the per-group heights and what
falls below the fold at 1440×900, 1440×1080 and 1280×800. The 1,745px figure is from one
capture at one width and should be confirmed across the sidebar's two widths (216px, and
180px below 1099px).

**2. Decide the mechanism.** Candidates, each with a different cost:

- **Collapse the lower groups.** Markets & tools, Trade management and Administration
  become disclosures that remember their state, leaving the diary loop always open. Keeps
  every destination in the tree; costs one click on the less-used groups.
- **Move destinations out of the sidebar** into the pages that own them — the pattern
  `/stocks/:symbol` now uses after [110](110-company-research-page-sections.md), and the
  one `/tools` already uses as a ruled index. Shortens the tree; costs discoverability for
  anything that loses its standing link.
- **Pin the account block to the bottom of the viewport** so Settings, Sign out and
  Preferences are never below the fold whatever the list does. Smallest change; does not
  address the 30-destination tree.

These are not exclusive and the third is probably correct regardless of the first two.

**3. Keep the command palette as the escape hatch**, and check whether the ruling changes
what it should contain.

**Verification:** `workspace-navigation.spec.ts` and `narrow-viewport.spec.ts` must pass —
note that three `workspace-navigation` cases are currently failing for unrelated reasons
(see the tracker's stale-test list) and must be characterised before this starts. Add an
assertion that the account controls are inside the viewport at 1440×900 without scrolling
the sidebar.
