# [113] Correct the research tool pages: clipped actions, ambiguous labels, card stacking

Status: needs-triage
Execution: todo
Published: 2026-10-06

Category: bug
Type: Design follow-up
User stories covered: Public research tools — Relative value, Seasonality, and the shared ToolShell surfaces.

## Observation

Found during the [whole-app page score](../../../docs/design/ui-page-score-2026-10-06.md).
`/tools` itself scored **84** — it is the ruled two-column list `DESIGN.md` specifies and
needs nothing. The individual tool pages drift from the system in the opposite direction.

### `/tools/relative-value` — 58/100

**Row action buttons are clipped.** The scenario table's action column is cut off by the
left panel's right edge and the buttons read **"Use this ro"**. Visible at 1440px, so this
is not a narrow-viewport case. Shared with [102](102-confirmed-markup-defects.md) item 7.

**Three buttons are all labelled "Fetch quote".** Confirmed in source: `c.fetch` is
rendered three times in `routes/relative-value.tsx` — once under the primary symbol column,
once under the comparison symbol column, and once as a filled button below both, beside
the text "The calculation keeps manual and fetched inputs separate." Nothing in the labels
distinguishes which symbol each affects, or what the third one does that the first two do
not.

**"Current ratio" and "Inverse ratio" are stated twice** — once in the ratio region below
the inputs, once again inside the Scenario prices panel.

**The ratio history chart renders a flat line in a large empty frame** when both prices
are equal. Honest, but the same low-information chart presentation that
[105](105-performance-chart-low-cardinality.md) addresses on the performance page; the two
should be resolved consistently.

### `/tools/seasonality` — 68/100

The content is the best data presentation in the app — a twelve-month table with character
and historical reasons per month, the current month highlighted, honest source boundaries.

**But the page is six stacked bordered cards**: Scope and source, Current month, Next
month, Monthly reference, Interpretation, Research capture. `DESIGN.md`: "`.panel` is the
default way to group content: a heading with a rule under it and content below, flush on
the canvas — no box, no shadow," and `.card` is for "a form, a dialog body, or content that
genuinely needs an edge." Six boxes down a reading page is the thing the Structural
Hierarchy Rule exists to prevent.

**"Monthly reference" is printed twice** — as the card heading and as the table caption.

**The monthly bar chart collides with its own labels.** Small negative values (Feb and Aug
at −0.01%) print their label across the zero line, and September's −0.72% label sits at or
past the left edge of the plot area.

### Shared

**`/tools/position-sizing` ships an empty Results card** — a bordered box with a "Results"
heading and ~60px of nothing, with no copy telling the reader what to enter to fill it.
Minor, but it is the first thing a guest sees on the most-linked calculator.

**`/tools/market-rotation` was not assessed in its populated state** — the harness had no
seeded snapshot. Its empty state is good (honest message plus scoped retry) and scored 71
provisionally. Any work here should capture the populated page first; it is the largest
route file in the app at 941 lines and the review has no coverage of it.

## Requested outcome

Bring the tool pages back to the panel-and-rule grouping the tool index already follows,
fix the clipped actions and the ambiguous labels, and resolve the duplicate headings.
Preserve every calculation, the guest-accessible behaviour, the source-boundary copy, the
Copy Markdown and research-capture paths, and the fixed-dataset disclosures.

## Implementation plan

**1. Fix the clipped scenario actions** on relative-value. Establish whether the cause is
the action column's width, the panel's overflow, or the table's minimum width, and put the
table in a named scroll region if it needs one.

**2. Disambiguate the three quote actions.** Each should name what it fetches. If the third
button genuinely does something the first two do not, its label must say so; if it is a
fetch-both, it should say that.

**3. Remove the duplicated ratio statement** and the duplicated "Monthly reference"
heading.

**4. Convert the stacked cards to panels.** Seasonality's six regions become headings with
rules, flush on the canvas. Keep an edge only where something genuinely needs one — the
Research capture form is the plausible exception, since `.card` is explicitly for forms.
Check the other tool pages for the same pattern while there; the review saw it on
Seasonality and partially on Relative value.

**5. Fix the bar chart labels.** Small-magnitude and negative labels must not overlap the
zero line or leave the plot area. Resolve consistently with
[105](105-performance-chart-low-cardinality.md) rather than inventing a second approach.

**6. Give the position-sizing Results panel an empty state** naming what to enter.

**7. Capture and assess `/tools/market-rotation` populated** before deciding whether it
needs work. Do not restyle it blind.

**Verification:** `tests/e2e/public-tools.spec.ts`, `position-sizing.spec.ts` and
`market-rotation.spec.ts` are updated and must pass. Add an assertion that no action
button on relative-value is clipped at 1440, 768 and 390 — a width comparison, not a
screenshot. Tool pages must keep working for guests, which is the property most at risk
when a page is restructured.

## Provisional acceptance criteria

- [ ] No action button on `/tools/relative-value` is clipped at 1440, 768 or 390.
- [ ] No two buttons on one tool page carry the same label for different actions.
- [ ] Neither the ratio figures nor the "Monthly reference" heading is stated twice.
- [ ] Seasonality groups its regions with headings and rules; any surviving `.card` is a form or genuinely needs an edge, and no card is nested in a card.
- [ ] Chart value labels do not overlap the zero line or extend outside the plot area at any supported width.
- [ ] `/tools/position-sizing` shows an empty state naming what to enter before results exist.
- [ ] `/tools/market-rotation` is captured and assessed populated; any change to it is justified against that capture.
- [ ] Every calculation result is unchanged; guest access, Copy Markdown, research capture and the fixed-dataset source copy all behave as before.
- [ ] Private actions stay secondary and preserve guest inputs across sign-in, per the tools access matrix.
- [ ] Verified in all three locales, light and dark, at 390/768/1440.
